The free skills

listing-description

Finds the hook and puts it in the headline. A viewing-order walk-through with one concrete detail per room, a facts block, bullets, social caption and SMS blurb, all within your MLS character limit. US fair-housing wording check; UK material-information check (price, council tax, tenure and lease, utilities, flood risk, restrictions).

lead-follow-up

A first reply that answers their question and asks one easy one, with a detail that isn't in the listing. Qualifying questions for buyers, sellers and landlords. Dated hot / warm / nurture sequences that switch channel and know when to stop. Covers the written buyer agreement before US tours.

offer-comparison

The same fields for every offer, estimated net with the working shown, escalation clauses only when triggered, certainty rated with a reason, timing fit, a neutral highest-and-best message, and a seller email under 300 words. Buyer "love letters" are left out, on fair-housing grounds.

  1. Download the files. On the GitHub page, click the green Code button, then Download ZIP. Unzip it and open the chat folder.
  2. Create your assistant in the app you already use:

Questions

Will AI listing descriptions get me in trouble with fair housing rules?

The listing-description skill describes the property, never the kind of buyer, and checks wording against HUD's advertising guidance (phrases like "perfect for a young family" or "exclusive neighborhood" are rewritten as features). You are still responsible for the final copy, so check it against your MLS rules and brokerage policy. See the guide fair housing words to avoid, and the ones that are fine.

Does it cover UK material information?

Yes. For UK listings it flags missing material information: price, council tax band, tenure and lease details, plus utilities, broadband, parking, and things like flood risk, restrictions and rights of way where they apply. The duty now sits under the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024, and the skill uses the industry's A/B/C checklist as a working standard.

Is it legal advice?

No. It drafts listings, messages, tables and checklists for a licensed agent to review. Contract dates, disclosure duties, agency and compensation points are prompts to check against the signed contract, your brokerage or firm's forms and local law.

Does it connect to my MLS, CRM or portal?

No. You paste notes, enquiries, offer terms or an MLS export, and it returns copy and tables you paste into your MLS, Rightmove or Zoopla feed, CRM or email. It doesn't log in to anything or post on your behalf.
